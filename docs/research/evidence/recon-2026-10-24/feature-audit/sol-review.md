[Official PR](https://github.com/pingdotgg/t3code/pull/2829), [pinned managed-tool source](https://github.com/pingdotgg/t3code/blob/27e0c684c585f955252d65f06206dd11a143bff5/apps/server/src/mcp/toolkits/orchestrator/tools.ts).

Status totals reconcile to 2,774 merged directly to main, 509 merged into stacked branches, and 644 closed-unmerged: **3,283 merged in total**. Keep these qualifications visible:

- Twenty product rows mean roster coverage and selected feature-family review, not all features of all twenty products.
- Current repository SHA, packaged stable/nightly version and observed runtime behaviour are separate evidence. GOAT's sampled dev source is not packaged-release proof. Experimental MonoCode remote documentation is not a runtime guarantee.
- Traycer's public client cannot establish its private host/backend guarantees. Empryo's public v2 source does not establish private v3 behaviour. These remain unavailable evidence rather than missing capabilities.
- `excluded` applies to the particular hosted gateway, fleet, relay, mobile or UI-port behaviour, not automatically to every feature of that product. OpenCodeX/GOAT/HarnessRouter can still provide narrowly eligible accounting or protocol ideas. Annotate this mixed scope in the matrix rather than relying solely on one scalar product status.
- Prefer `present in inspected production source` over `complete`, even for a narrow feature. Source presence does not close live acceptance.
