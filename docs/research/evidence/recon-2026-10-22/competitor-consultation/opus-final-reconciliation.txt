# Final reconciliation: Opus

I agree with all seven points. One clarification each on points 3 and 7, neither a disagreement. Nothing is left unresolved.

## AGREED

1. **Recovery PID risk (reviewed by Sol).** Recorded under the existing Phase 1E / X-7 restart and stop acceptance, not as a new card. It is backed by source and has not been reproduced at runtime.
   - A process may be terminated only on strong identity: the same PID plus its start time, executable, and attempt ownership, as far as the platform supports them. A command line alone is not identity.
   - A null or legacy owner that cannot be verified is never signalled, and its possibly live workspace and claim are not freed until reconciled. The consequence is that such an item stays in attention until a human resolves it.
   - The claim is released only after the old process is confirmed gone.
   - No rename migration. The honest label comes from the existing state projection.

2. **Cap refusal.** The refusal returns `{activeCount, limit}`, the reason, and which dimension was hit (per parent or per environment). "End the turn" is guidance to the model; the engine never forces an interrupt.

3. **Late background work.** Accepted.
   - The pinned result is immutable and never reopens.
   - Later work that is known stays visible, triggers reconciliation and attention, and keeps the workspace lease and capacity while it could still change files.
   - Capacity is never released just because a result was pinned.
   - Clarification: "known" means positively observed (`observed_live`). A provider with coverage `not_reported` does not hold capacity on that basis alone. Capacity is released on positive settlement or an explicit human Stop.
